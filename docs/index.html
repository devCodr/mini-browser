4 Ways to Reorder Your Tabs

Organize tabs your way without friction or complicated configurations.

1

Right-Click Context Menu

Right-click (or Ctrl+Click) on any tab in the top bar to reveal quick actions: Move Left, Move Right, Manage Sessions, or Close.

2

Fast Keyboard Shortcuts

Press Cmd + Option + ← to shift the active tab left, or Cmd + Option + → to shift it right instantly.

3

Pointer Drag & Drop

Click and hold any tab, then slide left or right. Other tabs smoothly part in real-time to preview your placement before dropping.

4

Session Manager (Cmd + M)

Open the management center to view all sessions. Use 1-click ↑ and ↓ arrow buttons, edit titles and badges, or delete with ease.

Documentation & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

🔑 macOS Keychain WebCrypto Key Notice

WhatsApp Web requires standard WebCrypto master keys for E2E encryption. WebKit safely delegates these keys to the macOS Keychain. Select "Always Allow" and enter your Mac password once to let WhatsApp store its session safely.

✉️ Google & Gmail Official Sign-In

MiniBrowser uses genuine Safari 18 User-Agent signatures to bypass Google automation warnings. Navigating to gmail.com routes straight to Google's official sign-in endpoint (accounts.google.com/ServiceLogin).

📁 Where is my session data stored?

All cookies, localStorage, and caches are stored 100% locally in ~/Library/Application Support/mini-browser/sessions/ on macOS. There is zero external telemetry.

🏷️ How do custom badges work?

Assign badges like W1, G1, or Work with custom colors to instantly spot accounts in the top tab bar. Edit badges anytime via Cmd + M.
